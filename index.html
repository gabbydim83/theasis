Welcome to Theasis

Comfort, sea view & a walkable Neos Marmaras location.

Theasis Luxury Apartments and Studios in Neos Marmaras, Sithonia, brings together two comfortable 30 m² studios — Theasis 4 and Theasis 5. Each has a large double bed, sofa bed, fully equipped compact kitchen, Smart TV, fast Wi‑Fi, a private terrace with sea view and free private parking, and can host up to 3 adults.

Within roughly 100 metres you can find supermarkets, brunch cafés, a taverna, pharmacy and local fish, meat and fruit shops. The centre of Neos Marmaras is about a 5-minute walk away, with sandy Paradisos Beach and its clear waters at almost the same walking distance.

30 m² each · Up to 3 adults · About 5 min walk to the centre & Paradisos Beach
